# iso-engine model and world conventions

What the engine and the demo expect from a model, and what the map does with it. Source of
truth in the repo: `docs/assets.md` ("Model conventions", "Authoring models with Blender",
"Maps"), `game/demo/src/objects.rs`, `game/demo/src/animals.rs`.

## Model files
- glTF 2.0 (`.glb`), Y up, metres, front +Z (Blender -Y), origin at the footprint centre on the
  ground. One mesh per file; colours from material base colours (no textures, no UVs, no
  normals: the loader derives flat normals).
- Opaque; back faces culled. Closed surfaces only.
- Keys `<family>.<name>`, files `assets/models/<family-dir>/<name>.glb`, every file referenced
  by `assets/manifest.ron` (a test fails on orphans).
- `engine_assets` tests every manifest mesh: lowest point within 2 cm of y = 0, origin inside
  the XZ footprint, no triangle wound against its normals, positive enclosed volume, skinned
  rest pose reproducing the bind pose (identity palette).

## Families (first part of the key) in the demo
| family | collides | ground shadow | other |
|---|---|---|---|
| `deco` | no | no | flowers, grass, crops, mushrooms, pebbles |
| `tree`, `vehicle` | footprint below 0.5 m | static, model extent | |
| `animal` | as a moving body | follows the animal | wanders, gait (rig names below) |
| anything else (`prop`, `plant`, `rock`, `building`) | footprint below 0.5 m | no | |

Colliders are one axis-aligned box per model: the XZ bounds of every vertex lower than 0.5 m,
turned and scaled with the placement. A gate that should be passable needs a gap between two
models, not one model with an opening.

## Rig joint names
- Any model: a joint named `spin` turns about the model's front axis (windmill sails).
- Animals: `body` (root), `head` (nods about X, nose down positive), `tail` (swishes about Y),
  `leg_fl`, `leg_fr`, `leg_bl`, `leg_br` (diagonal pairs swing together) or `leg_l`, `leg_r`,
  plus `wing_l`, `wing_r` for birds (flap about Z). Missing joints are skipped.
- Animal size, walking speed (0.45 x length, clamped 0.35-1.2 m/s), body radius and stride all
  come from the model's footprint: model at true size.

## Asset list
- `docs/asset-list.md` (generated by `tools/list_assets.py`) lists every asset with triangles,
  size, joints, map use and the commits that added and changed it. Refresh it in the item's
  closing commit; `--check` exits 1 when a manifest edit or an asset commit made it stale.

## Map placement (`assets/maps/demo_world.map`)
- `[objects]`: `key x y [facing] [scale]`, x/y in tiles (whole = tile corner, .5 = centre),
  facing N/NE/E/.../NW or degrees clockwise from north; unturned models face south.
- `[scatter]`: `L = ground key key ...` makes grid letter L lay that ground and put one of the
  keys on the tile with a per-tile random (but fixed) variant, yaw, scale 0.85-1.15, shade and
  offset. Letters `c` and `p` are reserved.
- Ground characters: `,` grass, `:` dirt, `_` cobbles, `%` field, `~` water (blocks), `.` wood,
  `+` tile; `#` tall wall, `=` low wall; `@` spawn.
